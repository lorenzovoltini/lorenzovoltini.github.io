Key achievements

In 3 years experience I have directly contributed to the assembly, integration, and test of 26 microsatellites (25 kg) and one medium-sized satellite (1000 kg).

I have participated and contributed to full satellite EVT campaign, prepared and updated 70+ A.I.T. procedures, and participated on-site to 3 launch campaigns. In total, I have successfully launched 24 satellite in less than one year.

I'm also co-author of 3 papers accepted in international conferences, one as main author and personally presented at the 33rd Space Flight Mechanics Meeting in Austin, Texas (USA).

Professional experience

Since November 2023, I'm working as space systems Assembly, Integration, and Test (A.I.T.) Engineer at OHB Italia S.p.A.

RAMSES (September 2026 - Today): Prepared A.I.T. procedures and procedures dashboard, participated in progress and review meetings. Responsible of defining and tracking A.I.T. activities, schedule, and requirements. Responsible of payload and scientific instrumentation A.I.T. systems engineering and acted as A.I.T. representative in payloads IRR, TRR, DRB. Participated and contributed to full EVT campaign (TVAC, EMC, vibrations, SVT) in ESA-ESTEC and to launch campaign in Tanegashima, Japan. Acted as deputy of A.I.T. manager when absent.
