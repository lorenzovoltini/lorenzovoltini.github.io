Key achievements

In 3 years experience I have worked on the assembly, integration, and test of 26 microsatellites (25 kg) and one medium-sized satellite (1000 kg).

I have participated and contributed to full satellite EVT campaign, prepared and updated 70+ A.I.T. procedures, and participated on-site to 3 launch campaigns. In total, I have successfully launched 24 satellite in less than one year.

I'm also co-author of 3 papers accepted in international conferences, one as main author and personally presented at the 33rd Space Flight Mechanics Meeting in Austin, Texas (USA).

Professional experience

Since November 2023, I'm working as space systems Assembly, Integration, and Test (A.I.T.) Engineer at OHB Italia S.p.A.

1) RAMSES (September 2026 - Today): Prepared A.I.T. procedures and procedures dashboard, participated in progress and review meetings. Responsible of defining and tracking A.I.T. activities, schedule, and requirements. Performed units and harness integration, supported functional tests, verified and organized inputs and set-up for A.I.T. activities. Responsible of payload and scientific instrumentation A.I.T. systems engineering and acted as A.I.T. representative in payloads IRR, TRR, DRB. Participated and contributed to full EVT campaign (TVAC, EMC, vibrations, SVT) in ESA-ESTEC and to launch campaign in Tanegashima, Japan. Acted as deputy of A.I.T. manager when absent. One medium-sized satellite launched in April 2028 with JAXA H3.

2) SATURN (February 2026 - April 2026): Supported program in implementing lessons learned in design and A.I.T. flow. 3 microsatellites launched in 2030.

3) IRIDE (November 2023 - September 2026): Performed full cycle of A.I.T. activities (mechanical integration, electrical/functional and optical tests, TVAC, EMC, mass and geometrical properties characterization, GSEs setup), prepared A.I.T. procedures, schedules, logbooks, and reports, participated in three launch campaigns (24 satellites) in Vandenberg (USA) with preparation for shipping, integration on launcher, final functional tests. Verified and organized inputs for A.I.T. activities, participated in progress and review meetings, and acted as deputy of A.I.T. manager when absent. 8 microsatellites launched in November 2025 with SpaceX Transporter-15, 8 microsatellites launched in March 2026 with SpaceX Transporter-16, 8 microsatellites launched in October 2026 with SpaceX Transporter-18.

4) EAGLET-2 (November 2023 - October 2026): Prepared A.I.T. procedures, performed mechanical and electrical integration, and supported functional tests. Contributed to A.I.T. systems engineering in final mechanical design review. Acted as A.I.T. manager deputy when absent. 2 microsatellites launched in February 2027 with SpaceX Transporter-19.
